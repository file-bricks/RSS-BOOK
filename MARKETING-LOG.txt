### 1. Target Personas & High-Intent Alignment

1. **`[PERSONA-01]` Privacy-Conscious News & Tech Readers:**
   - *Pain Point:* Reluctance to create yet another third-party cloud aggregator account (Feedly, Inoreader, NewsBlur) that tracks reading habits, injects promoted posts, or subjects reading feeds to server-side telemetry.
   - *Solution:* Direct browser-internal storage into Chromium bookmarks. 0 external network egress beyond direct feed fetches; zero telemetry, zero analytics.
2. **`[PERSONA-02]` Chromium Multi-Device Power Users:**
   - *Pain Point:* Need feed subscriptions synchronized across workstations, home PCs, and laptops without maintaining self-hosted servers (FreshRSS, Miniflux) or paying for cloud sync tiers.
   - *Solution:* Leverages the native cross-device bookmark synchronization built directly into Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave Sync, and Vivaldi. Feeds update locally and sync automatically through browser user profiles.
3. **`[PERSONA-03]` Extension & Local-First Software Engineers:**
   - *Pain Point:* Bloated extensions with hundreds of npm dependencies, obfuscated bundles, or background scripts that drain laptop battery.
   - *Solution:* Dependency-free Manifest V3 extension. Pure native JavaScript using standard browser APIs (`chrome.bookmarks`, `chrome.storage.local`, `chrome.alarms`, `chrome.notifications`). Zero bundling, 100% auditable code, service worker terminates after update cycle.
4. **`[PERSONA-04]` Information Curators & Research Archivists:**
   - *Pain Point:* Vendor lock-in where exporting feeds is difficult, lossy, or restricted.
   - *Solution:* Full OPML 2.0 import and export with single-decode entity protection and BOM stripping. Unique folder export generating Windows/OneDrive native `.url` internet shortcut files.
